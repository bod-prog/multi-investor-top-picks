dividend is $0.31, and buyback authority was increased by $50M on top of a
completed multi-year repurchase of 13,868,342 shares.

Three things stop that becoming an Overweight.

**The headline overstates the substance.** Total revenue rose 21% but **net
revenue before reimbursements rose 12%** — the difference is pass-through
costs carrying no margin. And **Q2 EPS of $0.60 merely matched consensus**; the
"topped estimates" framing came from other lines.

**Growth is structurally headcount-bound.** Utilization can move from 72 to 74
but not to 95; rates can rise 4% but not 20%. The engine is hiring credentialed
technical staff, which is linear, slow and competitive. Roughly 9–12% is close
to a permanent ceiling, and the FY26 guide of **+9–10% net revenue** says so.

**The company's own full-year margin guide sits below the quarter it just
printed.** Q2 realized EBITDA at **28.7%** of net revenues; FY26 guidance is
**27.8–28.1%**, and Q3 is guided to 28.0–28.5%. Raising full-year margin
guidance *to a level beneath the current quarter* is not the same thing as
raising margins, and the bull's framing did not survive that.

What decides it is consistency with the ruling this desk made an hour earlier.
**QLYS was rated Hold at ~21.9x forward earnings for 9–10% growth with a 45%
operating margin and a Rule of 40 of 54.5.** EXPO grows at the same rate, earns
a **28%** EBITDA margin, and trades at **~30.7x trailing**. Same growth, lower
margin, higher multiple. Rating EXPO above QLYS would require the difference in
category risk — Exponent faces no Wiz or CrowdStrike equivalent — to be worth
roughly nine turns of earnings. That may be true, but nothing here quantifies
